Leekcapper in Fortnite:
How to Find It and Use It

v43.10 referenceBattle RoyaleZero Build

The Leekcapper is a weapon found through random loot in Fortnite Battle Royale and Zero Build. Available loot sources and drop rates vary by mode, so check the matching table before you search. To complete “Board a Ship with the Leekcapper,” switch to the weapon and hold it in your hands as you board one of the ships marked on the map. This guide covers where to look, attack damage, charged smashes, durability and the four-step quest walkthrough.

01 / Find it

Where to Find the Leekcapper in Fortnite

Find the Leekcapper through random loot, using the sources listed for your current mode. The Battle Royale table includes Chests, Floor Loot, Rare Chests and Supply Drops; the Zero Build figures cover Floor Loot.

Match your mode to the table. Battle Royale lists four loot sources; Zero Build lists ground loot. An unlisted source does not mean a zero-percent drop rate.

Zero Build ground loot

The Leekcapper ground-loot figures are 0.22% for Zero Build and 0.34% for Zero Build Ranked. Keep these separate from the Battle Royale rates rather than applying one playlist’s numbers to another.

Is there a fixed Leekcapper location?

Spawns are random. A listed drop rate does not guarantee a pickup at a particular ship or landing location. Find the weapon first, then choose a marked ship for the quest.

02 / Use it

How to Use the Leekcapper

Use the fire input for normal swings. Hold the aim input to build up to two charges, then release it for a smash. The Leekcapper tables separate combo damage, smash damage, knockback and durability costs so you can compare each action.

The controls below use input actions rather than device-specific key bindings. Choose the action you want to compare, then check its damage and durability cost in the tables.

Normal swings and the four-attack combo

Press fire for a single swing, or hold fire to continue the combo. Its third attack deals 20 damage twice; that double hit is part of the 160 total. The final attack knocks players back approximately 10 meters. An airborne attack uses two hits with the same damage as the first two combo attacks, but a different animation.

Charge a smash

Hold aim to store up to two charges, then release the input to swing. The charge meter around the reticle and the electrical effect around the weapon indicate charging. For the Leekcapper smash figures above, the uncharged and fully charged versions have different damage and knockback. The listed distances are approximate.

Movement and durability

Holding the weapon provides increased speed and reduced stamina consumption. Sprint jumping gives extra height and the described fall-damage protection; do not treat that description as unlimited protection in every situation. The Leekcapper movement ability consumes durability while active, even without a successful hit.

03 / Board a ship

How to Complete “Board a Ship with the Leekcapper”

Equip the Leekcapper and hold it in your hands before boarding a marked ship. Follow the map below and try to stay toward the south of the island for convenient access to the marked ships.

Use the map markers to choose a qualifying ship. Four of the five qualifying ships are in the southern part of the island. Staying toward the south makes it convenient to approach a marked ship once you have the weapon.

Four-step quest walkthrough

  1. Find a Leekcapper.Use the loot sources for your mode. Acquisition is random, so finding the weapon comes before the boarding step.
  2. Choose a marked ship.Refer to the map above. Prefer staying in the southern part of the island for convenient access to the marked ships.
  3. Equip the weapon.Switch to the Leekcapper and have it in your hands before boarding.
  4. Board while holding it.Step onto a qualifying ship with the Leekcapper equipped. This is the supplied action for completing the objective.

04 / Check the basics

Leekcapper Not Dropping or Quest Not Counting?

If the weapon is not dropping, check your mode and loot source; finding it is random. If the quest is not counting, check the map marker and hold the Leekcapper as you board. Also check its durability, because the weapon disappears when durability reaches zero.

If the weapon is not dropping

A Leekcapper search can be unsuccessful because acquisition is random. Match your source to the mode-specific information above. The figures do not promise a drop after a set number of searches, so avoid treating an empty search as proof that the item is unavailable.

If the ship objective is not counting

  • Use one of the five ships marked on the map.
  • Switch to the Leekcapper before boarding and hold it in your hands.
  • Check that the weapon is still in your inventory; zero durability removes it.

These Leekcapper checks follow the boarding requirements. They do not establish a software-bug fix.

05 / Quick answers

Leekcapper FAQ

The key checks are your mode, random loot source, remaining durability, and whether you hold the weapon when boarding a marked ship.

Is there a guaranteed Leekcapper location?

The reference information describes random map spawns. It does not identify a location that guarantees a Leekcapper, so searching a marked area is not a promise that the item will appear there.

Can I find the Leekcapper in Ranked?

Yes. The supplied v43.10 figures include Battle Royale Ranked and Zero Build Ranked. Use the figures for your mode rather than treating all four mode listings as one loot pool.

How do I charge the Leekcapper?

Hold the aim input to build up to two charges, then release it to swing. Check the meter around the reticle and the electrical effect around the weapon as the charge builds.

Why did my Leekcapper disappear?

Its durability can run out. At zero durability, the Leekcapper breaks and is removed from your inventory. Successful attacks and the active movement ability consume durability.

Which ships count for the Leekcapper quest?

The five ships marked on the map count. Four are in the southern part of the island, making the south a convenient area to stay in while preparing to board.

Should I hold the Leekcapper when boarding?

Yes. Switch to the Leekcapper and hold it in your hands as you board a qualifying ship. This is the confirmed action used in the walkthrough above.

What if the Leekcapper quest is not counting?

Check that you chose one of the five marked ships and equipped the Leekcapper before boarding. These checks restate the boarding requirements; they are not a confirmed fix for a software bug.

Does “leek caper Fortnite” mean Leekcapper?

Yes. The reference information confirms that this search variant refers to the same item. Leekcapper, written as one word, is the spelling used throughout this guide.

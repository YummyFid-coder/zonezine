How to get the Kasane Teto skin

Follow the five illustrated steps from the lobby to the Outfit and its reactive style.

LOBBY → PASSES

Step 1: Start in the lobby

Sign in to the Epic account where you want the Outfit. Select Passes in the top navigation; the first screenshot outlines this entry in red.

PASSES → PASSES & FN CREW

Step 2: Browse the available passes

If the Passes area opens on Battle Pass, use the Passes & FN Crew entry near the bottom of that screen to browse other passes. The Battle Pass is separate from the Teto Music Mini-Pass.

MUSIC PASS → GET MUSIC PASS

Step 3: Review the Music Pass offer

Select TWINDRILL DIVA, then choose Get Music Pass at the bottom right. Review the 1,000 V-Bucks offer and your account balance on the following confirmation screen. Join Fortnite Crew is a separate subscription option; existing active members can use their included access.

CLAIMED → EQUIP

Step 4: Claim and equip Teto

Pass access immediately unlocks the base Kasane Teto Outfit. The reward screen can show Claimed and offer an Equip button; you can also select the owned Outfit in your Locker. This does not complete the remaining reward progression.

PASS PROGRESSION → FINAL REWARD

Step 5: Earn and select the reactive style

Continue earning XP toward the final pass reward, claim the reactive style, then select the owned Outfit’s style options in your Locker. The screenshot shows Reactive On selected on the final reward page and the captured account’s remaining page-unlock condition.

Choose Get Music Pass or Fortnite Crew

Follow the illustrated steps above: open Passes, browse Passes & FN Crew, and select the TWINDRILL DIVA Music Pass tile. The Get Music Pass button leads to the standalone offer. The large Join Fortnite Crew button opens a recurring subscription instead. On the purchase screen, check the product name, V-Bucks amount and account before confirming. An active Crew member should first open the included pass rather than buying another access route. Example: unlock the base Outfit, then equip it

Suppose your goal is to wear Teto immediately and you have enough V-Bucks for the standalone pass. Start at the Music Pass tile, review the 1,000 V-Bucks offer, and complete that purchase on the account you play. The expected result is access to the pass and the immediate base Outfit grant. On the reward screen, use Equip if it is offered; otherwise open the Locker and select Kasane Teto in your Outfit collection. The remaining pass entries still need progression. Unlock the final music-reactive style

The music-reactive option comes at the final tier. After getting access, earn XP and return to the pass to inspect the rewards that have become available. Epic’s pass system lets XP from Fortnite experiences advance active passes together, so playing Festival is not the only way to make progress. Claim unlocked rewards manually or use the pass’s auto-claim option. The captured final-page screen shows a gate of nine more reward claims or 20 earned levels for that account. Read the requirement on your own page before choosing the next task. Once the final style is earned, open the Outfit’s available style options in the Locker. Skin guide FAQ

Is Kasane Teto sold separately in the Item Shop?

The confirmed launch route is the TWINDRILL DIVA Music Mini-Pass. Its 1,000 V-Bucks price is for pass access and the immediate base Outfit grant. The pass can also appear as a Music Pass tile in the Shop. That tile sells pass access; a standalone Outfit offer would have its own contents and price.

Do I need XP before I can wear the base skin?

No. Getting the pass immediately unlocks the base Outfit. XP is relevant to the additional pass rewards, including the final music-reactive style.

Does Fortnite Crew include the Teto Outfit?

An active Crew subscription includes the Music Mini-Pass. Open the pass on that account to access its immediate Outfit reward. Crew is a paid, recurring subscription.

Do I have to play Fortnite Festival?

No. Epic’s progression system uses XP from Fortnite experiences to advance active passes. You can choose an XP-awarding experience you enjoy, then return to the pass to claim available rewards.

Why does the preview show a glowing Teto?

That can be the music-reactive style shown in a showcase or reward preview. Check whether your Locker has that style unlocked; the final reward is separate from the immediate base Outfit.

Does finding Teto in Battle Royale unlock the Outfit?

The announced island appearance and the Outfit purchase route are different parts of the collaboration. Visiting an island character does not replace getting access to the Music Mini-Pass.

Kasane Teto Fortnite Release Date

By ZoneZine editorial · Published November 14, 2026 · Updated November 15, 2026 · Source review: November 14, 2026

The Teto Music Mini-Pass launched November 12, 2026. Epic scheduled Teto and Cap’n Hatsune Miku’s Friendship Villa appearance for November 15. The supplied pass screen displays availability through January 20, 2027.

Announcement

Epic published the Teto Music Mini-Pass announcement on November 11, 2026, detailing the pass price, instant Outfit unlock and headline rewards.

Pass launch

The Music Mini-Pass launched on November 12, 2026. Epic’s French press release described it as available that day.

November 12: the Music Mini-Pass launch

Epic announced the TWINDRILL DIVA Music Mini-Pass on November 11, with access starting November 12, 2026. That is the relevant release date when your question is when the Outfit became obtainable through the pass. The launch offer immediately grants the base Teto Outfit, while further rewards follow pass progression. The Kasane Teto Fortnite skin guide covers the menu route and what happens after getting access. A story about the later island event is describing another part of the crossover, so check which release a headline is referring to.

November 15: Teto and Miku at Friendship Villa

Epic separately scheduled Kasane Teto and Cap’n Hatsune Miku to arrive at Friendship Villa on Crossbones Isle on November 15, 2026. The announcement gives November 15 as its calendar date, without specifying an exact arrival hour. It should not be described as the first opportunity to get the pass Outfit: the announced pass launch came three days earlier. Use the timeline controls above to switch between the announcement, pass launch and island appearance. Keeping those events separate answers the common question of why different pages attach different dates to the same collaboration.

January 20, 2027: the date displayed on the pass

The owner-supplied English TWINDRILL DIVA screen reads Available through JAN 20 2027. This is the displayed Music Pass availability date in that capture. The preceding Battle Pass screenshot reads December 4, 2026, but that deadline belongs to Battle Pass and should not be transferred to Teto’s Music Pass. When checking your own game, select the Music Pass tile first and read the date attached to that product. Use the displayed window to plan remaining rewards, while treating the exact cutoff hour and timezone as separate details.

What the announcements say about exact times

The announcement used here provides calendar dates, without a specific launch hour or timezone. The supplied availability screen also does not establish an exact cutoff time. There is therefore no reliable hours-and-minutes countdown to show. For example, a player deciding whether to finish a final reward on January 20 should check the current pass timer rather than assuming access lasts until their local midnight. The practical distinction is between a published date, the date visible in a captured interface, and a live countdown attached to the offer on your account.

Finding the pass during its availability window

Start in Passes, open the list of passes and Crew benefits, and select TWINDRILL DIVA. If the screen first shows Battle Pass, switch to the Music Pass tile rather than looking only in the Item Shop. Confirm the game is updated and that the signed-in account is the one you intend to use. If access was purchased already, review its transaction and pass status before attempting another purchase. For a purchasing decision, the Kasane Teto Fortnite price guide covers standalone access and Crew. For completion planning, use the Teto Music Pass rewards checklist.

Release date FAQ

What is Kasane Teto’s Fortnite release date?

November 12, 2026 is the announced Music Mini-Pass launch date. November 15 is the separate scheduled island appearance at Friendship Villa.

Why do some pages say November 15?

They may be discussing the Battle Royale island appearance with Cap’n Hatsune Miku, rather than the earlier launch of the pass and its Outfit reward.

When does the Teto Music Pass end?

The supplied English pass screenshot displays January 20, 2027. Its precise cutoff hour and timezone are not visible, so use the current in-game timer when planning a last-day purchase or claim.

Does the December 4 Battle Pass deadline apply?

No. That date appears on the separate Battle Pass screen. Teto’s TWINDRILL DIVA Music Pass has its own displayed availability window.

Is an exact release hour confirmed?

The cited announcement gives a date without an exact hour. A timezone conversion requires a source timestamp; a calendar date alone does not supply one.

Will the skin return to the Item Shop later?

The sources listed here do not announce a future shop date. A pass’s ending date is not evidence of a confirmed shop return, and it does not establish that a return is impossible.

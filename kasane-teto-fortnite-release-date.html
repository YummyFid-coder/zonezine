Kasane Teto Fortnite Release Date

By ZoneZine editorial · Published November 14, 2026 · Source review: November 14, 2026

The Teto Music Mini-Pass launched November 12, 2026. Epic separately announced a November 15 Battle Royale island appearance at Friendship Villa. These dates describe different parts of the collaboration.

Announcement

Epic published the Teto Music Mini-Pass announcement on November 11, 2026. The announcement is the source for the pass price, Outfit grant and headline rewards.

No exact hour or countdown is shown because the announcement used here does not establish one.

The confirmed timeline

Epic published its announcement on November 11. The Music Mini-Pass launch followed on November 12, and the official French press release described the pass as available that day. As of this guide’s November 14 editorial check, the announced pass launch date is in the past. The island appearance is still a separate scheduled event. This page therefore uses released language for the pass, while preserving scheduled language for content due later. It is a date guide based on published sources, not a live test of the current Fortnite servers or your account.

Why you may see two release dates

A crossover can arrive through several surfaces: a pass, an Outfit entitlement, an island character, a location or a shop offer. Search results often compress those into one headline. For Teto, the November 12 pass announcement and November 15 island plan explain why different dates can appear in otherwise related stories. Before sharing a date, identify exactly what the source is describing. If your question is when you can unlock the pass Outfit, the pass launch matters. If you want to visit the announced location, use the island date instead.

Is there a confirmed launch hour?

The announcement used for this guide gives calendar dates without an exact launch hour or a named timezone. We do not convert an unspecified date into a precise countdown. A timer displaying hours and minutes would imply a certainty the source does not provide. Readers in different regions should consult in-game notices and Epic’s current service communications for actual availability. A local calendar can also differ from the date attached to an announcement. When an exact timestamp is published, it can be converted transparently; until then, the honest answer is a confirmed date with an unconfirmed hour.

What to do if it is not visible

First identify the right destination in the game: you are looking for the named Music Mini-Pass rather than assuming every crossover item must be in the Item Shop. Confirm that your game is updated and that you are signed into the intended account. If there is a purchase or entitlement issue, use Epic’s support and transaction information rather than repeatedly making a payment. An announcement does not establish that every device, account or region is displaying the same screen at the same moment. We have not verified individual platform rollout times for this article.

End dates and future returns

Do not infer the pass deadline from the next announced event, another artist’s schedule or a social-media countdown. Check the expiry information actually displayed in Fortnite before buying access for rewards you still need to earn. This guide does not claim a verified end timestamp or a guaranteed future Item Shop return. A missing return date also should not be rewritten as a promise that something will never return. Purchase decisions are easier when confirmed launch information, a currently displayed deadline and future speculation are kept separate. Save a screenshot of the live offer if timing is critical to your decision.

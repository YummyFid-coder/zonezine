Use the three-page reward checklist

The table groups the 15 entries by their reported pass page. Use the image gallery to inspect each item, filter by page or type, and mark your collected rewards. Those marks are saved in this browser; they are your own collection notes and do not claim anything in the game. The base Kasane Teto Outfit is the immediate access reward. Earn XP in the Fortnite experiences you enjoy

Epic’s pass progression guidance says active passes advance together through XP earned in Fortnite experiences. That includes XP earned while playing Battle Royale; you do not have to stay in Festival because this is a Music Pass. After playing, open the pass and inspect your newly available rewards. Its displayed requirements are the useful reference for the next unlock. The base Outfit, a reward page, and a final style are different milestones, so do not use a character’s account level as a substitute for checking the relevant reward.

Claim rewards manually or turn on auto-claim

Rewards can be claimed as they unlock, and each pass has an auto-claim option for collecting the next available reward. Choose the approach that suits how you track progress. For example, a player collecting a particular Jam Track can review the unlocked choices and claim that track when available, then mark its card here. Someone who wants a simpler routine can use auto-claim and check the collection after each session. Purchasing rewards on one pass does not advance a different pass, so inspect the selected pass before spending currency on a direct reward purchase.

Compare Jam Tracks, emotes and the final style

BIRDBRAIN, Shut Up and Dance, Chronicles of Destiny and Sa-Sen! are the four announced Jam Tracks. Use the player above to hear original artist videos or streaming previews, then select the matching reward card. Birdbrain also appears as an emote: its dance entitlement is separate from the BIRDBRAIN Jam Track. The last Teto entry is the music-reactive Outfit style. Hue Futura is listed with several cosmetic uses, so its row should not be treated as several unrelated pass rewards. For a close look at the character itself, open the Kasane Teto Fortnite skin guide.

Plan a session around your remaining rewards

Before playing, pick the next item you want and note its current in-game requirements. After earning XP, reopen the Music Pass, claim what is available, and update this checklist. The supplied pass screen displays availability through January 20, 2027; the precise ending hour and timezone are not shown. Crew users also need to watch their subscription’s billing end date, because premium Music Pass claiming requires active access. The Teto pass price and Crew guide explains that distinction. Your own session length will depend on the rewards left and the XP you earn.

Pass rewards FAQ

Are all 15 entries granted immediately?

No. The base Outfit is the immediate grant. Additional cosmetics, tracks and the final reactive style are earned through the pass’s progression and claiming system.

Does Battle Royale XP count toward this Music Pass?

Yes. Epic says XP from Fortnite experiences advances all active passes together. Festival is an option, rather than a requirement for every session.

Can I gift the Teto Music Mini-Pass?

At the November 14 review, Epic’s support notice lists a technical issue preventing Music Mini-Pass gifting, with other passes unaffected. Check that notice and Fortnite Status for a resolution before planning a gift.

Does the pass give V-Bucks back?

The 15-entry reward list below contains no V-Bucks reward. Choose it for its Outfit, accessories, music and style rather than expecting the purchase price to return as currency.

Can I claim rewards automatically?

Yes. Use the pass’s auto-claim option to collect the next available reward as it unlocks. The checkboxes on this website only record your personal collection notes.

Can I finish after my Crew billing period ends?

Premium Music Pass rewards require active Crew access when that subscription is your access route. If its billing period ends before the pass, you will need an eligible access route to continue claiming premium rewards. Already claiming the base Outfit does not complete the pass.

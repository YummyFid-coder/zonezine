How the pages fit together

Start by finding the reward you care about most, then inspect its requirements in the game. A cosmetic appearing on the first page does not necessarily share the instant grant behaviour of the Outfit. Similarly, a completed-looking checkbox here does not mean you have earned the corresponding in-game slot. Our checklist stores your selections only on this device when browser storage is available. You can reset it whenever you want to audit your collection again. If you switch browsers or clear site data, you may need to rebuild that personal list.

Why Birdbrain appears twice

The Birdbrain emote and BIRDBRAIN Jam Track are separate reward types. An emote entry is not the same entitlement as a playable music track, even when the names refer to the same song. Check both entries if collecting music-related content is your main goal. The other tracks named in Epic’s announcement are Shut Up and Dance, Chronicles of Destiny and Sa-Sen! Artist credits can be reviewed in the official announcement and in the game. Do not assume that every song featuring Teto is included just because this is her headline pass.

Understanding the final reward

The final Teto entry is a reactive option for the Outfit, not a second unrelated character. If a showcase presents it as another look, check the style settings rather than searching for a completely separate skin name. Hue Futura is listed with several cosmetic uses in the secondary-source breakdown; inspect each relevant Locker category after unlocking it. This is another reason to track reward entries separately from the number of objects or settings you see in the Locker. Fifteen table rows do not automatically translate into fifteen interchangeable standalone shop purchases.

Plan around what you want to collect

For a small personal collection, mark your priorities before starting: character appearance, an accessory, a favourite track, or the final style. Then use the live pass screen to decide what remains. We do not publish a fixed completion-hour estimate or an XP shortcut here because neither has been verified for this guide. Progress can depend on the current requirements and your own play. Be especially careful with videos promising instant completion or requesting account access. A useful rewards guide should help you identify the target without pretending to know your account’s progress.

Buying and timing

Pass access is the purchase; completing its remaining rewards is the progression task. Check the price guide if you are choosing between V-Bucks and an existing subscription, and use the skin guide for the basic Outfit unlock. Our release-date page separates the initial pass launch from later island content. This checklist was compiled on November 14, 2026 and is not a live connection to the game. If a row differs from the current pass screen, follow the game and consult the linked sources. The character profile provides background for fans meeting Teto for the first time.

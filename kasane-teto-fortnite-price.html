What the 1,000 V-Bucks purchase includes

The announced amount buys access to the Teto Music Mini-Pass, including its immediate base Outfit grant and the opportunity to earn the remaining rewards. It is a pass price, rather than a separately announced Item Shop listing for the Outfit. To compare its contents with what you actually want, open the Teto Music Pass rewards checklist. The reward entries include cosmetics, four Jam Tracks and the final music-reactive style. Additional progression remains part of collecting those items after you buy access.

Choose standalone access or an active Crew subscription

Get Music Pass opens the standalone route; Join Fortnite Crew opens a paid recurring subscription. These buttons can appear on the same overview, so check which product the following confirmation screen names. If Crew is already active, open the included pass before paying separately. For someone considering a new subscription, compare its total benefits and next renewal date with the one-time pass purchase. Access through Crew can cover multiple active passes, but it also brings a billing period that may end before Teto’s pass availability window.

What happens if you cancel Fortnite Crew?

Epic says premium Music Pass rewards require an active Crew subscription when Crew provides your access. Canceling leaves access available until the current billing period ends. That date can be earlier than the pass’s January 20, 2027 availability date shown in the supplied screenshot. If your plan is to earn the final reactive style, consider whether you will still have an eligible access route when you reach and claim it. A subscription’s scheduled renewal, its current billing-period end, and the pass deadline are three different dates worth reading before choosing a route.

Worked example: a 600 V-Bucks balance

Suppose you want the standalone pass and currently have 600 V-Bucks. Against its 1,000 V-Bucks price, the balance shortfall is 400. Enter 600 in the planner above to see that difference. It does not mean the storefront sells a package containing exactly 400 V-Bucks: review the packages available on your platform and their final local-currency prices. After adding enough currency, return to Get Music Pass, review the selected product and confirm the purchase. The expected immediate result is pass access and the base Outfit; the final style still requires progression.

Read regional prices and receipts correctly

The supplied English Crew screen displays HK$76 per month. That is a regional subscription display, not a universal cash price for Teto or a receipt for the standalone pass. Store region, currency packages, platform and applicable taxes affect a cash comparison. A useful record includes the product name, account, amount and billing terms shown at confirmation. If the Outfit is your main goal, the Kasane Teto Fortnite skin guide shows the route into the Locker. For the buying window, consult the Teto release date and pass timeline.

Price guide FAQ

Is the Kasane Teto skin 1,000 V-Bucks?

That is the announced Music Mini-Pass price, with the base Outfit granted immediately through access. It is not a separate confirmed shop price for a standalone skin listing.

Is Teto free with Fortnite Crew?

The pass is included while the paid Crew subscription is active. Existing members should check that access first; joining Crew creates a recurring subscription rather than a free purchase.

Can I keep claiming premium rewards after Crew expires?

Premium Music Pass claiming through Crew requires active subscription access. Canceling preserves access until the end of the current billing period, not automatically until the pass ends. Review an eligible access route if you want to continue afterward.

Do I keep Teto if I cancel Crew?

Yes, once the Outfit has been granted and claimed, it remains yours. Epic says earned premium pass rewards are kept after cancellation. That is separate from access to unlock and claim additional premium rewards after the billing period ends.

Can the planner tell me the cash cost?

It calculates the V-Bucks shortfall against 1,000. Your storefront determines which currency packages are available and their actual prices, so use the checkout amount for a cash comparison.

What if I bought the pass and then joined Crew?

Review the transaction history and the current terms for that specific offer, or contact Epic support with the order details. A pass purchase and a Crew subscription are different transactions; do not assume a refund rule from another pass applies.
